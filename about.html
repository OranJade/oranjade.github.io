About Keonna

I am a senior at High Point University majoring in Computer Science with a concentration in cybersecurity. I chose this field because I have always been interested in understanding how technology works behind the scenes and how it can be protected from misuse. Cybersecurity, in particular, stood out to me because it combines technical skill, problem solving, and investigative thinking. High Point University has provided me with opportunities to develop my technical abilities through hands-on coursework, security labs, and collaborative projects that have strengthened both my knowledge and confidence in the field.

Through my academic experience, I have worked with programming languages such as C++ and Python and have completed projects involving vulnerability analysis, reverse engineering, and system security. These experiences have helped me develop persistence, attention to detail, and the ability to approach complex challenges with a thoughtful and analytical mindset. My concentration in cybersecurity has allowed me to explore areas that align with my career interests, particularly security analysis and investigative work.

Outside of academics, I am actively involved in campus and community organizations. I am a member of Zeta Phi Beta Sorority, Inc., where I participate in service initiatives and professional development activities. I have also volunteered with The Heroes Center, supporting efforts to connect veterans and their families with local resources. In my spare time, I enjoy watching musicals, spending time with friends and family, and continuing to explore my interests in technology. These experiences have helped me grow both personally and professionally and have shaped my goal of building a meaningful career in cybersecurity.
